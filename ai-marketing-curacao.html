AI Marketing FAQs

What is AI marketing? +

AI marketing means using artificial intelligence to improve parts of your marketing, such as research, content creation, analysis, personalisation, workflows and automation. At FUNkiness!, we combine those tools with marketing strategy rather than treating AI as a strategy by itself.

Can you help my business use AI? +

Yes. We look at how your business and marketing currently work, identify where AI can add real value and help you implement the right tools, workflows and processes.

Do I need to know anything about AI? +

No. You don't need to know your prompts from your APIs. We translate the technology into practical ways to improve the work you already do.

Do you offer AI training in Curaçao? +

Yes. FUNkiness! provides practical AI training for businesses and teams in Curaçao, focused on using AI effectively in everyday marketing and business workflows.

Can AI create all our marketing content? +

Technically, it can create a lot. That doesn't mean it should. We use AI to make content creation smarter and faster while keeping strategy, creativity, brand voice and human judgement firmly in the process.
