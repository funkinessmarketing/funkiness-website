SPIN THE FUN! terms

Black Friday 2026 campaign by FUNkiness!, Curaçao. Last updated: November 25, 2026.

1. Who runs this?

SPIN THE FUN! is organized by FUNkiness!, registered with the Curaçao Chamber of Commerce under no. 176072. Contact: sayhello@funkiness.ai or WhatsApp +599 9 675 1737.

2. When does it run?

From Thursday, November 26, 2026 until Sunday, November 29, 2026 at 11:59 PM Curaçao time. Entries after that moment do not count.

3. Who can join?

  • Businesses based on Curaçao only. The person entering does so on behalf of the business.
  • One valid entry per business. Entries with the same business name, email address, phone number, website or social media account count as the same business.
  • Joining is free. No purchase or payment is needed.
  • The person entering must be at least 18 years old.
  • Family members and partners of FUNkiness! cannot take part.

4. How does it work?

Fill in the form on funkiness.ai/black-friday and spin the wheel once. Every spin wins one prize. The prize is decided by our server at the moment you spin, not by your device.

5. Prizes and chances

  • 10 Killer Hooks: 18%
  • AI Power Pack: 18%
  • Content Rescue Kit: 18%
  • 30 Days of Ideas: 18%
  • Mystery FUNky Gift: 18%
  • Marketing SOS: 10%. A maximum of 5 Marketing SOS prizes is given out during the whole campaign, with a maximum of 2 per day. When none is available, the spin lands on one of the five digital gifts, each with an equal chance.

Digital gifts are delivered right away on screen and by email. They are for use by the winning business and may not be resold.

6. Marketing SOS

FUNkiness! reviews one website, Instagram account or Facebook business page of the winning business and delivers: one observation of what already works, three specific improvements and one action the business can start with right away. Delivered as a personal document of max one A4 page or a personal video of max three minutes; FUNkiness! chooses the format. Not included: a full marketing plan, a competitor analysis or a follow-up meeting.

The Marketing SOS is delivered by Friday, December 11, 2026. We email the winner. If the winner does not reply within 14 days after our email, the prize expires.

7. THE ULTIMATE FUNky MAKEOVER

One main prize, drawn on Monday, November 30, 2026 from all valid entries. The draw is done by a script that picks a random entry and records the number of entries, the time of the draw and a fingerprint of the entry list, so the draw can be checked afterwards. The winner is informed by email.

Included: one new creative concept, one new AI image, one hook, one matching caption, a short explanation of how to use it and one round of changes, for one existing social media post or ad of the winning business. Not included: a full marketing strategy, a complete ad campaign, video production, more than one concept or unlimited changes.

We email the winner. The winner has 14 days after our email to reply. We then agree on the delivery date together. If the winner does not reply within 14 days, we draw a new winner.

8. Exclusion

FUNkiness! may exclude entries that do not meet these terms, for example a business not based on Curaçao, fake details or more than one entry per business. An excluded entry does not take part in the main prize draw.

Prizes cannot be exchanged for cash or for another prize.

9. Your details

We use the details you fill in to run this campaign, deliver your prize and contact you about it. By joining you also receive the FUNky Letter, our newsletter with marketing and AI tips, at the email address you fill in. You do not need to tick a separate box. Every edition has an unsubscribe link, and you can leave the list at any time. More in our privacy policy.

We keep your entry details until your prize is delivered and the main prize is drawn, and for no longer than three months after the campaign ends. After that we delete them. If you accept advertising cookies, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok may receive a signal that you took part, without your name, email or phone number. Details are in our privacy policy and cookie policy.
