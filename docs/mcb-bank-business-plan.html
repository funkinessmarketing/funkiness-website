1. Executive Summary

FUNkiness! is a full-service marketing agency based on Curaçao, owned and founded by Daisy Kuipers. The agency combines marketing strategy, AI integration, social media management, content creation, and campaign execution for businesses on Curaçao, including hotels and resorts, restaurants, real estate agencies, vacation rental owners and managers, and banks and financial services.

FUNkiness! operates on two revenue tracks: (1) local, on-island full-service marketing engagements built around each client's specific needs, and (2) scalable digital products and subscriptions sold online, starting with a downloadable AI prompt kit for vacation rental hosts and a recurring social media management subscription currently in development.

The business is self-funded by the owner and is not seeking external financing. This account is requested to process online payments for FUNkiness!'s digital products and to support the agency's day-to-day business operations.

3. Products and Services

A. Full-service marketing engagements (local, Curaçao)

Custom marketing strategy, AI integration, social media management, content creation, and campaign execution for businesses on Curaçao. There are no fixed packages; each engagement is scoped to the client's size, sector, and goals.

B. Island Host AI Prompt Kit, digital product ($27 one-time)

A downloadable set of 12 AI prompts for vacation rental hosts on Aruba, Bonaire, and Curaçao, covering listing descriptions for major booking platforms and tools to improve bookings and guest reviews. Sold as an instant digital download via Gumroad.

C. The Spark Plan, recurring subscription (in development)

A monthly social media management subscription for businesses that need structural, ongoing content and strategy support. Anchor pricing is $500 to $750 per month depending on scope.

D. Free lead-generation tools

Two free assessment tools (a Social Media Scan and a Marketing Strategy Scan) that give prospective clients a personalized report on their marketing and AI readiness. These generate leads for the paid services above and do not themselves produce revenue.

4. Market Analysis

Industry: Marketing services, with a growing emphasis on AI-assisted content and strategy.

Local market context: Curaçao's tourism sector welcomed over 1.1 million stay-over visitors in 2023, the highest number in the island's history (Curaçao Tourist Board, 2023 Annual Report), with tourism accounting for approximately 30% of the island's GDP. This sustains steady demand for marketing services among hotels, restaurants, real estate agencies, and vacation rental businesses.

Target market: All businesses on Curaçao, with particular focus on hotels and resorts, restaurants, real estate agencies, and vacation rental owners and managers. The digital product line also targets vacation rental hosts across Aruba and Bonaire.

Competitive positioning: Most marketing services on Curaçao follow conventional, templated approaches. FUNkiness! differentiates through a strategy-first approach with practical AI integration built in from the start.

5. SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Founder brings professional marketing and AI experience from the Netherlands
  • Early positioning around AI-driven marketing, a topic most local competitors have not yet addressed
  • Low fixed overhead, online-first operating model
  • Owner's personal savings remove short-term revenue pressure during ramp-up

Weaknesses

  • New brand with no local track record yet
  • Small team: one full-time owner plus one freelance contractor
  • No local full-service clients under contract as of this writing

Opportunities

  • Marketing on Curaçao remains largely traditional and templated, leaving room to differentiate
  • Strong, growing tourism sector sustains demand from hospitality and real estate businesses
  • Shift toward AI-powered search creates a positioning angle competitors have not yet claimed
  • Digital product line can expand beyond Curaçao to Aruba, Bonaire, and further

Threats

  • Small total addressable market for local services on a single island
  • Local economy is partly dependent on tourism performance
  • Competitors could adopt a similar AI-driven positioning over time

6. Marketing and Sales Strategy

  • Direct outreach: personal outreach to specific local businesses, using the free assessment tools as a value-first opener.
  • Content marketing: a bilingual (English/Dutch) website and blog, including content on the shift toward AI-powered search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews).
  • Social media: active presence on Instagram and TikTok.
  • Digital product distribution: online sales through Gumroad, expanding the product line to additional sectors once initial sales validate the model.
  • Referral and reputation: word-of-mouth within Curaçao's close-knit business community, building on early client results.

10. Projection of Business Income

These figures are the founder's own conservative estimate. The retainer price point is grounded in published market research for marketing agency pricing, adjusted down to reflect a new brand entering a small local market with no track record yet. The growth pace assumes a realistic, gradual client acquisition rate rather than uninterrupted monthly growth.

Year 1 revenue assumptions

  • Local full-service engagements, custom pricing (average $850/month per client): the core of the business. Published market research on marketing agency retainers puts small-business pricing between $1,500 and $5,000/month in the U.S. market, with light single-channel engagements starting around $500 to $1,500/month. FUNkiness! anchors below that U.S. range, consistent with its existing Spark Plan pricing ($500 to $750/month for a narrower, social-only scope), reflecting a new, unproven brand in a small local market. Growth target of one net new client roughly every two months, starting in month 3, reaching 5 active clients by month 12. Estimated Year 1 total: $25,500.
  • The Spark Plan, $500/month founding rate: one founding client signed around month 6. Estimated Year 1 total: $3,500.
  • Island Host AI Prompt Kit, $27/sale: a gradual ramp from 2 sales/month in the first quarter to 8 sales/month by the fourth quarter, as outreach and content marketing build momentum. Estimated Year 1 total: 60 units, $1,620.

Basis for projection

This is a start-up-phase business, so the projection is a growth target rather than a historical baseline, and it is deliberately conservative. It reflects the outreach strategy already underway: direct, personal outreach to a shortlist of Curaçao businesses, opening with the free AI & Marketing Scan before proposing a custom engagement. Rather than assuming uninterrupted monthly client growth, the projection assumes a new client roughly every two months, which allows time for delivery, referrals, and realistic sales cycles between signings. The Spark Plan and Prompt Kit lines are modeled separately and even more conservatively, since they depend on Prompt Kit traction building first.

12. Break-Even Analysis

Base monthly operating costs during the early ramp-up period (before freelance support begins) are estimated at approximately $150 to $200 per month, covering software subscriptions, hosting, and email services. A single local client at the average $850/month retainer covers this cost several times over. Based on the growth target of one net new client roughly every two months, with the first client signed around month 3, FUNkiness! is projected to reach operational break-even in month 3 of its first year, well before the freelance support cost is added later in the year.
