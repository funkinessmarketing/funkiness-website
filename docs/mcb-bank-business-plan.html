1. Executive Summary

FUNkiness! is a full-service marketing agency based on Curaçao, owned and founded by Daisy Kuipers. The agency combines marketing strategy, AI integration, social media management, content creation, and campaign execution for businesses on Curaçao, including hotels and resorts, restaurants, real estate agencies, vacation rental owners and managers, and banks and financial services.

FUNkiness! operates on two revenue tracks: (1) local, on-island full-service marketing engagements built around each client's specific needs, and (2) scalable digital products and subscriptions sold online, starting with a downloadable AI prompt kit for vacation rental hosts and a recurring social media management subscription currently in development.

The business is self-funded by the owner and is not seeking external financing. This account is requested to receive all business income, both online digital product sales and payments from local clients, and to support the agency's day-to-day business operations.

Her background covers brand strategy, campaign management, digital marketing, and applied AI. FUNkiness! was founded on the observation that marketing on Curaçao is still largely traditional, and that there is a clear opportunity for a marketing partner that combines sharp strategy with practical AI tools. Daisy is fully dedicated to the business and has personal savings in place to support its growth, giving her the time to build FUNkiness! properly without financial pressure to compromise on quality or pricing. Starting late October 2026, marketer Berdien Renes joins as a freelance contractor based on Curaçao, adding capacity for content production and client delivery as the client roster grows.

3. Products and Services

A. Full-service marketing engagements (local, Curaçao)

Custom marketing strategy, AI integration, social media management, content creation, and campaign execution for businesses on Curaçao. There are no fixed packages; each engagement is scoped to the client's size, sector, and goals.

B. Island Host AI Prompt Kit, digital product (XCG 48.33 one-time, USD 27)

A downloadable set of 12 AI prompts for vacation rental hosts on Aruba, Bonaire, and Curaçao, covering listing descriptions for major booking platforms and tools to improve bookings and guest reviews. Sold as an instant digital download via Gumroad.

C. The Spark Plan, recurring subscription (in development)

A monthly social media management subscription for businesses that need structural, ongoing content and strategy support. Anchor pricing is XCG 895.00 to XCG 1,342.50 per month (USD 500 to 750), depending on scope.

D. Free lead-generation tools

Two free assessment tools (a Social Media Scan and a Marketing Strategy Scan) that give prospective clients a personalized report on their marketing and AI readiness. These generate leads for the paid services above and do not themselves produce revenue.

4. Market Analysis

Industry: Marketing services, with a growing emphasis on AI-assisted content and strategy.

Local market context: Tourism remains a major driver of Curaçao's economy, sustaining steady demand for marketing services among hotels, restaurants, real estate agencies, and vacation rental businesses.

Target market: All businesses on Curaçao, with particular focus on hotels and resorts, restaurants, real estate agencies, and vacation rental owners and managers. The digital product line also targets vacation rental hosts across Aruba and Bonaire.

Competitive positioning: Most marketing services on Curaçao follow conventional, templated approaches. FUNkiness! differentiates through a strategy-first approach with practical AI integration built in from the start.

5. SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Founder brings professional marketing and AI experience from the Netherlands
  • Early positioning around AI-driven marketing, a topic most local competitors have not yet addressed
  • Low fixed overhead, online-first operating model
  • Owner's personal savings remove short-term revenue pressure during ramp-up

Weaknesses

  • New brand with no local track record yet
  • Small team: one full-time owner plus one freelance contractor
  • No local full-service clients under contract as of this writing

Opportunities

  • Marketing on Curaçao remains largely traditional and templated, leaving room to differentiate
  • Tourism remains a major driver of the local economy, sustaining demand from hospitality and real estate businesses
  • Shift toward AI-powered search creates a positioning angle competitors have not yet claimed
  • Digital product line can expand beyond Curaçao to Aruba, Bonaire, and further

Threats

  • Small total addressable market for local services on a single island
  • Local economy is partly dependent on tourism performance
  • Competitors could adopt a similar AI-driven positioning over time

6. Marketing and Sales Strategy

  • Direct outreach: personal outreach to specific local businesses, using the free assessment tools as a value-first opener.
  • Content marketing: a bilingual (English/Dutch) website and blog, including content on the shift toward AI-powered search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews).
  • Social media: active presence on Instagram and TikTok.
  • Digital product distribution: online sales through Gumroad, expanding the product line to additional sectors once initial sales validate the model.
  • Referral and reputation: word-of-mouth within Curaçao's close-knit business community, building on early client results.

10. Projection of Business Income

Currency and period. All amounts in this plan are stated in Caribbean guilders (XCG), the currency of the account. Prices that are set in US dollars are converted at the exchange rate of October 1, 2026: USD 1.00 = XCG 1.79 (the official fixed rate of the Centrale Bank van Curaçao en Sint Maarten). The annual projections follow calendar years, starting with 2026. FUNkiness! began operating in August 2026, so 2026 is a partial first year of five months (August to December 2026); 2027 is the first full calendar year.

These figures are the founder's own conservative estimate. The retainer price point is set relative to the founder's existing Spark Plan pricing, reflecting a new brand entering a small local market with no track record yet. The growth pace assumes a realistic, gradual client acquisition rate rather than uninterrupted monthly growth.

Basis for projection

This is a start-up-phase business, so the projection is a growth target rather than a historical baseline, and it is deliberately conservative. It reflects the outreach strategy already underway: direct, personal outreach to a shortlist of Curaçao businesses, opening with the free AI & Marketing Scan before proposing a custom engagement. Rather than assuming uninterrupted monthly client growth, the projection assumes a new client roughly every two months, starting a few months into operation rather than immediately, which allows time for delivery, referrals, and realistic sales cycles between signings. The Spark Plan and Prompt Kit lines are modeled separately and even more conservatively, since they depend on Prompt Kit traction building first.

12. Break-Even Analysis

Base monthly operating costs during the early ramp-up period are estimated at XCG 315.00 per month (approximately USD 175), covering software subscriptions, hosting, and email services. A single local client at the average retainer of XCG 1,521.50 per month covers this cost several times over. From August to October 2026, monthly revenue from Prompt Kit sales (XCG 96.66) is below monthly costs (XCG 326.46), a monthly shortfall of XCG 229.80 that is covered by the owner's working capital buffer. From November 2026, when the first local client is expected, monthly revenue of XCG 1,714.82 exceeds monthly costs of XCG 490.07, giving positive monthly cash flow from November 2026 onward. Over the full 2026 period the business is projected to close with a net result of XCG 1,760.10.
