Branding & Campaign FAQs

What does a branding agency do? +

A branding agency helps define how a business should be positioned, understood and recognised. At FUNkiness!, we focus on brand positioning, strategy, voice and creative direction, then connect that thinking to your wider marketing.

Does FUNkiness! create branding for businesses in Curaçao? +

Yes. FUNkiness! works with businesses in Curaçao on brand positioning, brand strategy, brand voice and creative direction.

Can you create a marketing campaign for our business? +

Yes. We develop campaign strategy, creative concepts and rollout plans around launches, promotions and other commercial moments.

Can you help reposition an existing brand? +

Yes. You don't need to start from scratch. We look at what already works, identify what has become unclear or outdated and develop a sharper direction from there.

Do you also execute campaigns? +

Yes. Depending on the campaign, FUNkiness! can handle strategy, creative direction, social media and content, and coordinate the wider execution needed to bring the idea to life.
