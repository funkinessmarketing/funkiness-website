Privacy Policy

Plain language, not legal filler. Here's exactly what we collect, why, and who we share it with.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

FUNkiness! ("we", "us") is based in Curaçao. This page explains what personal data we collect through www.funkiness.ai, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, email us at sayhello@funkiness.ai and we'll explain it properly, no legalese.

What we collect,
and why.

We only collect what you give us directly, through one of these:

CONTACT FORM

Getting in touch

Name, email, company and your message. Used to reply to you. Sent through Formspree, a third-party form service, to our inbox.

FREE SCAN

AI & Marketing Scan

Name, email, company, sector, phone number, Instagram handle and your answers to the scan questions. Used to generate and email you a personalised report, and logged to a private Google Sheet as a backup so your submission never gets lost.

FREE QUIZ

AI Superpower Quiz

Name, email, company, sector and your quiz answers. Sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate your personalised result, then emailed to you.

NEWSLETTER

The FUNky Letter

Your email address and the language you pick. Used only to send you the FUNky Letter, and stored in a private Google Sheet. Unsubscribe anytime by replying "stop" to any edition, and we remove you from the list.

EVERY VISIT

Basic site analytics

If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics to understand which pages get visited and how the site is used, in aggregate, not tied to your name. You can decline this in the cookie banner.

Who we
share it with.

We don't sell your data. We use the following services to run FUNkiness!, and your data may pass through them:

  • GoDaddy / Titan Email, to send you scan reports and quiz results.
  • Google Sheets, as a backup log for scan submissions.
  • Anthropic (Claude API), to generate your personalised quiz result.
  • Formspree, to deliver contact form messages to us.
  • Vercel, our hosting provider, which processes standard web traffic data (like IP addresses) to serve the site.
  • Google Analytics, only if you accept cookies.

How long
we keep it.

We keep your data only as long as needed to respond to your enquiry or deliver the report or result you asked for, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your
rights.

You can ask us at any time to see what we have on file for you, correct it, or delete it. Email sayhello@funkiness.ai and we'll sort it out, usually within a few days.

Cookies.

See our Cookie Policy for the full list of what we use and how to manage your preference.

Questions?

Email sayhello@funkiness.ai. This policy is written in plain language to be genuinely useful, not to replace legal advice. If you need a formal compliance review, talk to a lawyer familiar with Curaçao's data protection rules.
