Cookie Policy

Short version: we use as few cookies as possible, and none that track you personally without asking first.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

A cookie is a small file your browser stores when you visit a website. Here's what www.funkiness.ai uses.

ALWAYS ON

Essential

One small entry in your browser's local storage to remember that you've seen the cookie banner, so we don't ask you again. No personal data, can't be turned off, and doesn't track you across other sites.

OPTIONAL

Analytics (Google Analytics)

Only set if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. Helps us see which pages get visited and roughly how, in aggregate, not tied to your name or email. You can decline this and the site works exactly the same.

Managing
your choice.

You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, which brings the cookie banner back. Most browsers also let you block cookies entirely in their settings.

See our Privacy Policy for what we do with any data collected.
